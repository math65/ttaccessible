# Turkish glossary

Catalogue: `App/ttaccessible/tr.lproj/Localizable.strings`. No native reviewer yet: the
catalogue was drafted, not reviewed by a Turkish speaker. Corrections are welcome.

## Conventions

- Address the user formally (**siz**): « sıradasınız », « başlatın ».
- Quotes: straight double quotes: "%@".
- Ellipsis: `…` (one character).
- Percentages: the sign comes first, « %50 ».
- Menu items and window titles are in title case: « Kanal Oluştur », « Kaydedilmiş Sunucular ».
