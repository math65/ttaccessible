# French glossary

Catalogue: `App/ttaccessible/fr.lproj/Localizable.strings`. Reviewer: Mathieu Martin.

## Conventions

- Address the user as **vous**, never tu.
- Write French from scratch; don't follow the English sentence word for word.
- Quotes: « » with a space inside: « %@ ».
- An ordinary space before `:`, `?` and `!`, as in the rest of the catalogue.
- Ellipsis: `…` (one character), not three dots.
- Percentages: a space before the sign, « 50 % ».
- Menu items and window titles take a capital on the first word only: « Créer un canal ».
- The Return key is « Retour », as in Apple's French documentation and the help book; « Entrée » means an audio input.
