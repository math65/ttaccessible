Send and read messages

You can write to everyone in your channel, to one person in private, or — with the right permission — to the whole server.

Send a message to the channel

  1. Press Command-3 to put the cursor in the message field.
  2. Type your message.
  3. Press Return, or click Send.

To read back through the conversation, press Command-2.

You must be in a channel to write. Otherwise tt-Accessible answers You must join a channel to send a message.

Send a private message

  1. Select the person in the channel tree.
  2. Control-click, then choose Send Private Message. The command is also offered as a VoiceOver action, and in the Connected Users window.
  3. Type your message, then press Return.

To reopen your private conversations later, press Command-E. In the Private Messages window, Command-1, Command-2 and Command-3 take you to the conversation list, the history and the message field. Each conversation shows whether the person is online, and marks unread messages.

Send a message to everyone on the server

  1. Press Command-B.
  2. Type your message, then click Send.

Broadcasting requires the matching right on your account. To stop receiving broadcasts from someone, see Adjust what you hear from each person.

Copy a message or follow a link

  • To copy the selected message, press Command-C. The command is also in the shortcut menu of both chat lists.
  • Web addresses in messages are detected automatically. Click one to open it, or reach it with the VoiceOver actions rotor, where each link is offered as Open link.

Save a conversation

Press Shift-Command-S, then choose where to save the file.
