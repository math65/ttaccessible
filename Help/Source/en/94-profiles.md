A profile is a complete, separate copy of your configuration: its own saved servers, settings, sound
packs, chat history and passwords. Each profile runs in its own instance of tt-Accessible, so you
can be connected to two servers at once, or keep a work identity apart from a personal one.

The name of the current profile appears in the title of the TeamTalk Servers window.

## Create a profile

1. Choose tt-Accessible > New Instance, or press Shift-Command-N.
2. Enter a name, then click **Create & Launch**. A separate instance opens with its own settings.

The name Default is reserved.

## Switch to another profile

1. Choose tt-Accessible > Manage Profiles.
2. Select a profile in the list, then click **Launch** or press Return.

The Status column shows which profile is **Current**, which are **Running**, and which is the
**Default** one.

## Copy, rename or delete a profile

In the Profiles window, select a profile, then do one of the following:

- **Copy it:** click **Duplicate**, then enter a name. Its servers, passwords, settings, chat
  history and sound packs are copied.
- **Rename it:** click **Rename**, then enter a new name. Nothing is moved or copied.
- **Delete it:** click **Delete**, or press the Delete key, then confirm.

Deleting a profile is permanent: its servers, settings, sound packs, chat history and passwords go
with it.

You can't duplicate or delete a profile while an instance is running with it — quit that instance
first. The profile you're currently using can't be deleted from that same instance; do it from
another one.
